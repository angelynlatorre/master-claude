🎯 What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic, designed to be helpful, harmless, and honest. Unlike a simple chatbot, Claude can reason, write, code, analyse, roleplay, and learn within a conversation. Mastering Claude means knowing how to talk to it — the art of prompting.

🗺️ Your Learning Path

  • Beginner → Understand what Claude is, its capabilities, and how to write basic prompts
  • Intermediate → Learn prompt engineering: roles, context, formatting, personas
  • Advanced → Chain prompts, use system prompts, build workflows, and go agentic

💡 Why This Matters

The difference between a beginner and an expert Claude user isn't intelligence — it's knowing how to prompt. Studies show well-structured prompts produce outputs 5–10× more useful than vague ones.

Pro tip: Think of Claude like a brilliant colleague. The more context, clarity, and constraints you give them, the better the output.
